Built for the stuff you forget you have

Three things Stockroom does so you don't have to think about it.

Scan once, stock forever

Point your phone at a barcode or a receipt. Stockroom fills in the name, quantity, and expiry date for you.

Reminders at the right time

Get a nudge when something hits your low-stock line or is close to its best-before date, not a daily digest of everything.

Shared with the household

Everyone sees the same list. Add an item from the shop, and it shows up on the shared shelf at home.

From the shop to the shelf in three steps

  1. Add what you own

    Scan, type, or import from a spreadsheet. Start with the shelves you use most.

  2. Set your low-stock levels

    Tell Stockroom how many you want to keep on hand. The defaults are sensible.

  3. Let it watch the shelves

    When something runs low or expires, it lands on your list. Tick it off when you buy it.
